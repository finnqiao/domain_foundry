Stage 02

Two sentences, and one is the test.

This is how a non-technical user supplies acceptance evidence without ever being asked to write an acceptance case.

Say one thing you'd log — exactly how you'd type it. That becomes the first test of the app.

peated dram, iodine and orchard fruit, 12 year, neat

And one more, a different kind of thing.

opened the Ardbeg 10 tonight, about two-thirds left

Good. The first one shapes the design. The second I'm holding back. I'll replay it after the app is built, and tell you honestly whether it filed.

Why hold one back

The repository rule is that the generator cannot author its own judge. Both sentences are the user's; only one informs the design, so the other is a genuine held-out check.

It also supplies the two acceptance tasks the Foundry pipeline requires, without the research-methods form the target user would abandon.

Stage 03

Cited, tiered, and priced.

Every claim the design rests on names its source and how far that source can be trusted. Nothing here is the model's unlabeled recall.

What I read about whisky

Whiskybase — community whisky databasedomain exemplar

Serious whisky tracking separates the distillery, the bottling, and the individual bottle, each with stable identity. Tasting notes are structured nose / palate / finish with a score, not one free-text blob.

W3C PROVauthoritative

A dram is an observation of a bottle at a time; a correction preserves the original record rather than overwriting it.

PostgreSQL — Constraintsauthoritative

Stable identities and relationships are explicit and enforceable, not repeated text fields.

WCAG 2.2authoritative

Keyboard access, focus, reflow, labels, status and error recovery are release requirements.

Held-out check. Your second sentence — “opened the Ardbeg 10 tonight, about two-thirds left” — replayed through the same router and updated that bottle to open, 66% left. Both of your sentences work. If one hadn't, this is where it would say so plainly.

Why it files now

The pack's routing rule is built from researched vocabulary (dram, peated, neat, iodine, cask), not from field labels like session name.

The tokenizer reads dimensioned terms, so 12 year and 46% are visible where 5x5 and 100kg once weren't.

And it stays correctable

“actually it was the 12, not the 10” amends the dram and keeps the original interpretation as a revision, so the correction becomes regression evidence.

Every promise on this page is auditable.

The sources are real registry entries. Whiskybase sits in knowledge/source-registry.yaml as a domain exemplar, tiered and license-labeled. The spec is real: examples/showcase/whisky-tasting/spec.yaml validates and compiles today. The visual world is real, because those eight swatches are the spec's own tokens rather than a palette invented for this page.

What is not yet real is the bridge that produces the spec unaided from the user's sentence. That is the work the plan phases in, and this page is the bar it must reach.
