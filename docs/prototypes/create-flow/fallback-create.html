Filed — but with your vocabulary only, not a researched model.

A researched build would know that peated, 12 year, and neat are three different kinds of fact (a malt characteristic, an age statement, and a serving style), and would separate the bottle from the pour. This one keeps them together in one card.

Honest confidence

71%, not 86%. The number reflects a rule built from two sentences rather than a researched vocabulary, and the disposition reads saved, review if wrong instead of auto-applying silently.

Still correctable

“no, the 12 not the 10” works here exactly as it does in the keyed build. Correction and provenance are trusted primitives, not premium features.

The floor is the product too.

Most people meeting this project will not have a key configured on their first run. Demo mode is what they judge the whole idea by, so it has to file their first sentence, keep their words verbatim, and be straight about its ceiling.

The plan holds the keyless path to a real number: the fifty-interest suite runs entirely in this mode, and its pass rate is a blocking gate that ratchets upward every phase.
